A little thank you

Your 9th drink is on us.

Ask at the bar for a Queenie’s loyalty card. Collect eight stamps, then enjoy your ninth eligible drink free. Ask our team which purchases earn a stamp.

Redeem your free drink on standard cocktails, wines, beers, single spirits with a mixer or soft drinks.

Signature cocktails, Prosecco and Champagne are excluded from free drink redemption.
